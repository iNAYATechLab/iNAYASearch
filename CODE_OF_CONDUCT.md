# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

iNAYASearch is committed to maintaining a respectful, constructive, and inclusive open-source community.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to communicate professionally and treat other contributors with respect.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss technical disagreements objectively.
- Focus on ideas, implementations, and evidence rather than individuals.
- Accept reasonable differences of opinion.
- Provide useful and honest technical feedback.
- Respect project maintainers, contributors, and community members.
- Protect private and sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or development.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Malicious behavior toward contributors or project infrastructure.
- Trolling intended to provoke or disrupt productive discussion.
- Any other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unreasonably hostile.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreements are normal in software development.

When disagreements occur:

1. Clearly explain the technical concern.
2. Provide evidence, tests, benchmarks, or documentation when appropriate.
3. Consider alternative approaches.
4. Keep the discussion focused on the project.
5. Follow the final decision of the maintainers unless the decision is reopened through an appropriate project process.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project maintainers through an appropriate private communication channel.

Do not publicly disclose private or sensitive information about another person.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible actions include:

- Requesting that inappropriate behavior stop.
- Removing inappropriate content.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation in serious or repeated cases.

Actions will be based on the circumstances and severity of the situation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue discussions, pull requests, documentation, community discussions, and other project-related spaces where participants represent or interact with the iNAYASearch community.

## Good-Faith Participation

This document is intended to support a productive technical community.

Contributors are encouraged to participate in good faith, assume positive intent where reasonable, and resolve disagreements through constructive technical discussion.
